Customer Data Platform — Retention Specification

Draft 3 · generated 2026-09-04

1. Scope

This document specifies how long the Customer Data Platform retains each class of record, what triggers deletion, and how deletion is evidenced. It applies to all environments including staging, which has historically been treated as exempt.

Anything not named in §3 is out of scope and continues to follow the general corporate retention schedule.

2. Definitions

Collection event
The moment a record first enters the platform, whether by API, batch import, or manual entry.
Deletion
Irreversible removal from primary storage, all replicas, and all backups taken after the deletion request.

3. Retention periods

3.1 Personal data

All personal data is retained for a period of ninety (90) days from the date of collection, after which it is deleted automatically by the retention worker. Records under legal hold are exempt for the duration of the hold.

Where a customer requests earlier deletion, the platform must action the request within thirty (30) days, and may not wait for the ordinary expiry.

3.2 Derived and aggregate data

Aggregates computed from personal data are retained indefinitely, on the basis that they contain no identifying information. Aggregates over cohorts smaller than fifty individuals are treated as personal data.

4. Deletion mechanics

The retention worker runs hourly. It selects expired records, writes a tombstone, and issues deletes against primary storage and each replica in turn. Backups are not rewritten; instead the tombstone is replayed when a backup is restored.
